Make It Fillable

Turn your branded Loan Inquiry Form into a PDF your clients can type into, check off and sign.

Enter your access code

You'll find the access code in the instructions that came with your Etsy purchase.


Before you start

  1. Add your branding in Canva: your name, photo, contact info and headings.
  2. In Canva, click Share → Download, choose PDF Standard, include both pages, and download.
  3. Upload that PDF below and click Make Fillable.
  4. Email the new PDF to your client. They can fill it out in any free PDF reader.

Upload your form

Your form stays private: it's converted right here in your browser and is never uploaded anywhere.

Tips

  • Keep the form lines and checkboxes where they are. Change only your branding and headings.
  • Upload the PDF straight from Canva. Don't print it to PDF or run it through other tools first.
  • Before sending, open your fillable PDF and try typing in a box to make sure it looks right.
  • Your clients can use Adobe Acrobat Reader (free), Preview on Mac, or a web browser.
  • This form collects sensitive information like Social Security numbers. Ask clients to return it securely, for example through an encrypted email or a secure upload link.
